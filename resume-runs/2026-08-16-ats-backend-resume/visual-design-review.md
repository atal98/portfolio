# Visual design review - Modern Technical companion

## Intended hierarchy

1. Name, target role, contact, and labelled public links.
2. Summary with backend and AI-workflow positioning.
3. Grouped technical skills.
4. Reverse-chronological experience.
5. Education.

## Style rules applied

- One restrained navy-blue accent with neutral ink and muted metadata.
- Strong left-aligned header and thin divider.
- Compact two-column technical-skills table only; no sidebar, photos, icons, or decorative graphics.
- Important content remains in normal document flow.
- Former-client and product names remain anonymised; unconfirmed metrics remain excluded.
