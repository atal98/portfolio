# Draft resume - ATS Backend Engineer

**Positioning assumption:** general Backend Engineer applications in India, emphasising Python, Django, FastAPI, AWS, and AI-enabled workflows. This is not job-posting tailored.

## Content choices

- Use a single column and standard ATS headings.
- Lead with role positioning and a short, evidence-backed summary.
- Put experience before education.
- Keep former employers but anonymise unapproved client/product names.
- Omit exact metrics until they can be defended and disclosed.
- Omit certification and public-profile sections until missing details are supplied.
