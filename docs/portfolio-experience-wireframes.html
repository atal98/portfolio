ANNOTATIONS / SPEC

01Bell is the first actual control. A semantic button sits over the 3D object; no preparatory CTA.
02Gate composition: [3D] gate, path, lanterns, foliage. [DOM] only utility navigation and accessible instruction.
03GSAP owns gate/camera timing; XState accepts one completion event per transition. “Skip” lands at the corridor state safely.
04Corridor is a spatial hub, not a level-select UI. Six chapters share one door system with subtle physical variation.
05Door focus exposes a concise label and Enter action. It must work through click, tap, Enter, and Space—not hover alone.
06Introduction is a warm study composition. Content grows inside the semantic drawer only once the visitor is in the room.
07Mobile: preserve the gate silhouette; enlarge bell to at least 44×44 px; shorten travel; remove only decoration.
08Mobile: foreground door and contextual label stay legible; Map provides direct chapter access and escape from the cinematic route.
